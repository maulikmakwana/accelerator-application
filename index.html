Animation studios within the Western Cape already have the talent and the stories. What's often missing is structured access to the commercial pathways that turn creative potential into sustainable growth.This accelerator is built to close that gap - for studios ready to take the next step.dfsfs this is test !!

WHAT YOU'LL GAIN

Structured diagnostics across commercial model, production pipeline, market positioning, finance and partner readiness, resulting in your own growth roadmap.

Virtual sessions with internationally recognised animation practitioners, curated through Triggerfish's global network, plus in-person sessions at the Creatives Hub.

Production mentors, plus commercial and business mentors, matched to whether you're building service work, original IP, or both.

All studios receive a stipend to attend the Durban FilmMart (DFM); 3 studios receive a stipend to attend Stuttgart's International Trickfilm Festival (ITFS), Animation Production Day (APD) and the FMX Conference.

With global studio founders, streaming executives, distributors and African animation leaders, plus a studio-facing insights brief from every session.

Designed around your production schedule, reducing travel while keeping engagement consistent over the eight months.

A refined pitch deck, production package and finance readiness memo, with direct feedback from industry decision-makers.

Is this for you?

This programme is for growth-stage, founder-led animation studios based in the Western Cape that are ready to scale. You should be able to say yes to the following:

  • Founder-led animation studio, operating as a registered business
  • Minimum 3 years of operational experience
  • Evidence of year-on-year growth, or strong growth potential
  • A clear intent and strategy to scale, whether through service work, original IP, or a hybrid model

Female-led studios and founders from underrepresented groups are especially encouraged to apply.

HOW TO APPLY

Complete the online application, supported by the following documents:

  • Company profile, SARS tax clearance and registration documentation
  • Founder and core team biographies
  • Studio portfolio and production reel / showreel
  • Overview of services, productions, IP or specialist capabilities
  • A short motivation statement including your growth ambitions, target markets, partnership goals and scaling challenges
  • Diversity and inclusion information (optional but encouraged), including women-led ownership or leadership representation
